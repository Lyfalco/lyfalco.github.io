2. Free version and Dustbye Premium

The core features of the app are free. Dustbye Premium unlocks unlimited rooms and tasks, color families, alternate app icons, focused cleaning mode, the weekly summary and other Premium features. The current list is shown on the purchase screen in the app. Premium is available in two ways:

  • Yearly subscription: renews automatically.
  • Lifetime: a one-time purchase that does not renew. It can be shared with Family Sharing.

Prices are shown in the app before purchase, in your App Store currency.

3. Subscription terms

  • Payment is charged to your Apple Account when you confirm the purchase.
  • The subscription renews automatically for the same period and price unless it is cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period; your account is charged for renewal within the 24 hours before the period ends.
  • You can manage or cancel your subscription on your iPhone under Settings › [your name] › Subscriptions. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current period; Premium stays active until then.
  • Refunds are handled by Apple under Apple's policies: reportaproblem.apple.com.

4. Lucky wheel discounts

The app may occasionally show a discount wheel for Premium. A discount you win is valid for 24 hours and is offered on the purchase screen only during that time. A discounted yearly subscription renews at the discounted price until it is cancelled. Spinning the wheel is free and no purchase is required.
